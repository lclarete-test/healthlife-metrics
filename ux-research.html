This work helps you

  • Find usability and comprehension problems

Decisions

Test the experience before assumptions become expensive.

UX research can support early concepts, live products, redesigns, and continuous improvement.

01

Can people use it?

Identify where users hesitate, misunderstand, abandon, or fail to complete important tasks.

02

Does it make sense?

Test terminology, content, navigation, information hierarchy, and mental models.

03

What should improve first?

Prioritize changes using user evidence, business goals, and product analytics.

Possible scope

Research across the product lifecycle.

Studies can focus on patients, caregivers, healthcare professionals, staff, or other users of healthcare technology.

What we can test

  • Healthcare websites and apps
  • Patient portals
  • Clinical and professional tools
  • Concepts, prototypes, and new features
  • Information architecture and content
  • Accessibility and product experience

Methods

  • Moderated usability testing
  • Unmoderated usability testing
  • Concept and prototype testing
  • UX interviews and surveys
  • Card sorting and tree testing
  • Benchmarking and mixed-method studies

Outputs

Clear evidence for product teams.

Usability findings

Observed problems, supporting evidence, severity, and affected tasks.

User journey

Goals, touchpoints, barriers, workarounds, and experience gaps.

Priority actions

Changes organized by user impact, business relevance, and effort.

Research assets

Study materials, clips or quotes, findings, and decision-ready recommendations.
