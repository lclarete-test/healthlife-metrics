What it is for

Evidence that improves the product experience.

01

Test the idea

Evaluate concepts, value propositions, workflows, and prototypes before investing further.

02

Improve performance

Increase usability, task completion, adoption, retention, and conversion.

03

Prioritize changes

Identify the navigation, content, accessibility, and product improvements with the greatest impact.

Products evaluated

Research across the healthcare digital experience.

Patient and Caregiver Products

Websites, apps, portals, education tools, enrollment, scheduling, and intake.

Clinical and Professional Tools

Telehealth, clinical tools, professional dashboards, and decision-support experiences.

Staff and Administrative Systems

Operational workflows, administrative systems, handoffs, and internal tools.

Concepts and Prototypes

Early concepts, paper prototypes, wireframes, interactive prototypes, and live products.

Methods

Choose the method that matches the product question.

The approach can be exploratory, evaluative, quantitative, or a focused combination.

Foundational and evaluative

  • Discovery and user interviews
  • Contextual inquiry and field studies
  • Diary studies and workflow research
  • Mental models and jobs-to-be-done
  • Journey maps and service blueprints
  • Concept, feature, and preference testing
  • Value-proposition and desirability testing

Usability and information architecture

  • Moderated and unmoderated usability tests
  • Remote, in-person, mobile, and cross-device tests
  • Task-based and think-aloud testing
  • Onboarding and form testing
  • Open, closed, and hybrid card sorting
  • Tree testing and first-click testing
  • Navigation, findability, and taxonomy testing

Specialized evaluation

Test clarity, accessibility, and measurable performance.

Content and Comprehension

Health literacy, readability, terminology, instructions, error messages, consent forms, trust, and credibility.

Expert Review

Heuristic evaluation, cognitive walkthrough, accessibility review, inclusive design, UX audit, and content audit.

UX Benchmarking

Task success, time on task, error rate, SUS, UMUX-Lite, SEQ, and comparative benchmarks.

Behavioral Analytics

Funnels, conversion, clickstream, heatmaps, session recordings, retention, and A/B testing.

Detailed deliverables

Evidence connected directly to product action.

Healthcare UX Research Report

Usability Issues and Severity Matrix

XLSX

Annotated Screens and Recommendations

PDF

Journey Map or Service Blueprint

PDF

Steps, channels, frontstage and backstage interactions, pain points, and opportunities.

UX Benchmarking Scorecard

Task success, efficiency, perceived ease, error rate, and benchmark comparisons.

Prioritized Product Backlog

XLSX

Recommended changes ranked by user impact, business impact, confidence, and effort.

Research Materials

DOCX

Research plan, discussion guide, usability tasks, moderator guide, and screener.
