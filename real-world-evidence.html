Understanding RWE

From real-world data to a focused research question.

Real-world data describes health status and healthcare delivery in routine settings. Sources can include electronic health records, insurance claims, patient registries, and suitable patient-generated data. RWE is the evidence derived from analyzing these data; the FDA definition focuses on the use and potential benefits or risks of a medical product.

The source alone does not establish the strength of the evidence. The research question, study design, data quality, and analysis determine what conclusions can be supported.

How we support your team

Project approach

Define the question. Assess the data. Document the evidence.

Plan and assess

  • Agree on the intended decision, population, and research question.
  • Review available evidence, data access, and source suitability.
  • Define methods, deliverables, and specialist responsibilities.

Analyze and interpret

  • Check data quality and document analytical choices.
  • Separate reported experience, observed patterns, and clinical outcomes.
  • Report limitations and questions requiring further study.

Scope and collaboration

Match the work to the intended use.

Patient insights and market research can support an evidence program, but do not on their own establish treatment effectiveness or safety. Clinical outcome studies, causal comparisons, and regulatory submissions require a separately agreed study design and appropriate clinical, epidemiological, statistical, and regulatory expertise.

Data access, privacy, ethics review where applicable, and a suitable analytical environment are defined before any transfer or analysis. For an initial discussion, share a description of your question and available sources through our contact form.
