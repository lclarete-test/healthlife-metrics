Research areas

Focused around the question your team needs to answer.

Needs and Behaviors

Needs, beliefs, attitudes, routines, information sources, and behavioral drivers.

Patient Journeys

Stages, touchpoints, handoffs, pain points, information needs, and opportunities.

Barriers and Access

Practical, emotional, clinical, organizational, and financial barriers to action.

Segmentation and Personas

Meaningful patient and caregiver groups based on behaviors, needs, attitudes, and context.

Concept and Message Testing

Relevance, clarity, credibility, differentiation, comprehension, and intended response.

Patient Profiling

Profiles that connect lived experience, behaviors, needs, and strategic implications.

Participants and methods

Research grounded in lived experience.

We recruit the people closest to the decision, including patients, caregivers, and family members.

Participants

  • Patients and caregivers
  • Family members and care partners
  • People managing chronic conditions
  • People considering a treatment or product
  • Newly diagnosed or recently treated patients

Methods

  • In-depth interviews
  • Dyads, triads, and focus groups
  • Online communities and diary studies
  • Ethnography and contextual research
  • Journey and critical-incident interviews
