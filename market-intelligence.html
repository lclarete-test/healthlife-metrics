Evidence

Sources and methodology are shown separately.

Research sources establish the evidence base. Analytical methods turn that evidence into a decision-ready market view.

Sources

  • Government and public health datasets
  • Regulatory documents
  • Clinical and academic literature
  • Industry reports and market databases
  • Company websites and financial filings
  • Investor presentations
  • Product documentation and pricing
  • Press releases and market announcements

Methodology

  • Desk research and evidence triangulation
  • Top-down and bottom-up market sizing
  • Growth forecasting and trend analysis
  • Competitor and product benchmarking
  • Pricing and positioning analysis
  • Market-movement tracking
  • Scenario analysis
  • Opportunity scoring and risk assessment
