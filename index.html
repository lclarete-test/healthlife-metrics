How we work

Human judgment stays in the loop.

AI can accelerate discovery and synthesis. It cannot decide which evidence is trustworthy, understand every human context, or own the consequences of a recommendation. Our work combines efficient tools with rigorous review and direct human interpretation.

  1. Frame

    Define the decision, the unknowns, and the evidence threshold.

  2. Investigate

    Gather primary and secondary evidence with a clear source trail.

  3. Interpret

    Connect findings, test assumptions, and identify meaningful gaps.

  4. Decide

    Deliver a concise view of the options, implications, and next moves.
