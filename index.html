Data privacy and confidentiality

Agree on data handling before the research starts.

A project begins with the intended use of the data, permissions, and confidentiality requirements. These shape what we collect, who can access it, and how findings are reported.

Confidentiality and research participation

Nondisclosure and data-use terms can be agreed during scoping. For primary research, the scope defines participant consent, recording permissions, and how responses will be shared. Reporting should avoid unnecessary identifying details.

HIPAA and project-specific requirements

Before any work involving protected health information, HIPAA applicability, business associate requirements, and a suitable data environment must be confirmed. Public, aggregated, or appropriately de-identified data is considered where it can answer the question.

Patient and HCP data handling, retention, access, and any other applicable privacy requirements are defined for the specific project before data is transferred.

For an initial quote, describe the question and available sources. Do not include patient-identifiable records in the website form. HIPAA applicability guidance from HHS →
