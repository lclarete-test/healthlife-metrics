What it is for

Evidence for better healthcare decisions.

01

Understand workflows

Map how care is delivered, coordinated, documented, and supported in practice.

02

Explain adoption

Understand clinical, operational, financial, and organizational drivers of adoption.

03

Improve ideas

Test concepts and messages with the people who will recommend, deliver, or use them.

Research areas

Focused around the question your team needs to answer.

Clinical and Operational Workflows

Roles, handoffs, routines, information flows, bottlenecks, and workarounds.

Decision-Making

Referral, prescribing, treatment, purchasing, access, and care delivery decisions.

Barriers and Adoption

Clinical, organizational, financial, technical, and workflow barriers to action.

Product and Technology Use

Needs, requirements, usability, trust, implementation, and ongoing use.

Segmentation and Personas

Meaningful HCP and staff groups based on roles, needs, behaviors, and context.

Concept and Message Testing

Relevance, clarity, credibility, differentiation, comprehension, and intended response.

Participants

Research with the people who deliver healthcare.

The stakeholder mix is selected for the decision and may include clinical, operational, and commercial roles.

Participants

  • Physicians and medical specialists
  • Nurses and advanced practice providers
  • Pharmacists and allied HCPs
  • Clinical and administrative staff
  • Practice and service-line managers
  • Healthcare executives and decision-makers

Qualitative methods

  • In-depth and semi-structured interviews
  • Dyads, triads, and focus groups
  • Online communities and diary studies
  • Ethnography, netnography, and observation
  • Contextual inquiry and workflow shadowing
  • Journey and critical-incident interviews
  • Co-creation workshops and social listening

Quantitative methods

Measure, compare, and prioritize.

Surveys and Profiling

Online or telephone surveys, audience profiling, and awareness or perception tracking.

Segmentation

Needs-based, behavioral, and attitudinal segmentation.

Concept and Message Tests

Concept, message, claims, satisfaction, and experience measurement.

Choice and Pricing

MaxDiff, conjoint, discrete choice, Gabor-Granger, and Van Westendorp.

Detailed deliverables

Concrete outputs your team can use.

HCP Research Findings Report

PPTX or PDF

Findings, evidence, implications, recommendations, and executive summary.

Workflow or Decision Journey Map

PDF

Stages, roles, handoffs, needs, decisions, barriers, pain points, and opportunities.

HCP and Staff Segmentation Profiles

PDF or PPTX

Distinct groups, defining characteristics, behaviors, needs, and strategic implications.

Concept or Message Testing Scorecard

XLSX or PDF

Performance by audience and evaluation criterion, with clear priorities.

De-identified Insight and Quote Library

XLSX or DOCX

Searchable themes, observations, and supporting participant language.

Research Materials

DOCX, XLSX, or survey export

Discussion guides, questionnaires, screeners, and supporting instruments.

Only the methods and deliverables required to answer the research question are included in the project scope.
