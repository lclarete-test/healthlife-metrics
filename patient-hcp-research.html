What it is for

Human evidence for healthcare decisions.

01

Understand people

Identify unmet needs, attitudes, behaviors, motivations, and lived experiences.

02

Explain decisions

Understand access, adoption, treatment, referral, and workflow decisions across the journey.

03

Improve ideas

Test concepts and messages before product development, launch, or communication.

Research areas

Focused around the question your team needs to answer.

Needs and Behaviors

Needs, beliefs, attitudes, routines, information sources, and behavioral drivers.

Decision-Making

Treatment, referral, prescribing, purchasing, access, and care decisions.

Barriers and Adoption

Practical, emotional, clinical, organizational, and financial barriers to action.

Journey Research

Stages, touchpoints, handoffs, pain points, information needs, and opportunities.

Segmentation and Personas

Meaningful audience groups based on behaviors, needs, attitudes, and context.

Concept and Message Testing

Relevance, clarity, credibility, differentiation, comprehension, and intended response.

Participants

Research across the healthcare ecosystem.

The stakeholder mix is selected for the decision—not limited to patients alone.

Stakeholders

  • Patients and caregivers
  • Physicians and nurses
  • Pharmacists and allied HCPs
  • Clinical and administrative staff
  • Practice managers
  • Healthcare decision-makers

Qualitative methods

  • In-depth and semi-structured interviews
  • Dyads, triads, and focus groups
  • Online communities and diary studies
  • Ethnography, netnography, and observation
  • Contextual inquiry and shadowing
  • Journey and critical-incident interviews
  • Co-creation workshops and social listening

Quantitative methods

Measure, compare, and prioritize.

Surveys and Profiling

Online or telephone surveys, audience profiling, and awareness or perception tracking.

Segmentation

Needs-based, behavioral, and attitudinal segmentation.

Concept and Message Tests

Concept, message, claims, satisfaction, and experience measurement.

Choice and Pricing

MaxDiff, conjoint, discrete choice, Gabor-Granger, and Van Westendorp.

Detailed deliverables

Concrete outputs your team can use.

Patient & HCP Research Report

PPTX or PDF

Findings, evidence, implications, recommendations, and executive summary.

Patient, Caregiver, HCP, or Staff Journey Map

PDF

Stages, touchpoints, needs, decisions, barriers, pain points, and opportunities.

Audience Segmentation and Persona Profiles

PDF or PPTX

Distinct groups, defining characteristics, behaviors, needs, and strategic implications.

Concept or Message Testing Scorecard

XLSX or PDF

Performance by audience and evaluation criterion, with clear priorities.

De-identified Insight and Quote Library

Searchable themes, observations, and supporting participant language.

Research Materials

DOCX, XLSX, or survey export

Discussion guides, questionnaires, screeners, and supporting instruments.
